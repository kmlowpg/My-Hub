My Hub

My Hub is a private, single-user personal organiser. It brings tasks and calendar events from the owner's own Google Calendar, Gmail and notes into one place so they can be viewed and managed on the owner's phone and computer.

It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It runs on the owner's own computer.

  • Calendar: view, add, edit and delete the owner's own events.
  • Gmail: read-only, used to spot tasks and dates in incoming mail. My Hub never sends, deletes or changes email.
